# Exercise 10: Data encryption using Azure key vault (Optional)

In this exercise, you will create your own encryption key to protect the data in your storage account using a **customer-managed key**. When you specify a customer-managed key, it is used to protect and control access to the underlying key that encrypts your data. Customer-managed keys offer greater flexibility and granular control over access policies. 

**Data encryption** is the process of translating data into another form or code so that access is strictly limited to only those entities possessing the correct decryption key or password. 

### Task 1: Enable Customer-Managed Keys for Storage Account Encryption

1. Navigate back to your **hands-on-lab-<inject key="DeploymentID" enableCopy="false"/>** resource group and select the storage account named **parts<inject key="DeploymentID" enableCopy="false"/>**.

   ![](media/lab8_01.png)
   
1. From the left navigation pane, under the **Security + networking** section, select **Encryption**.

   ![](media/lab8_02.png)
   
1. On the **Encryption** pane, select **Customer-managed keys** as the **Encryption type (1)** and choose the **Select from key vault** option for the **Encryption key (2)**. Click the **Select a key vault and key (3)** link located next to the key vault and key fields.

   ![](media/lab8_03.png)
   
1. On the **Select a key** panel, select **Key vault** as the keystore type and click the **Create new key vault** link.

   ![](media/ex5-task4-03.png)
   
1. You will be redirected to the **Create a key vault** page. Provide the following configuration details:

   - **Subscription**: Select your active subscription **(1)**.
   - **Resource group**: Select **hands-on-lab-<inject key="DeploymentID" enableCopy="false"/>** **(2)**.
   - **Key vault name**: Enter **appmod-keyvault<inject key="DeploymentID" enableCopy="false"/>** **(3)**.
   - Click **Next (4)**.
  
    ![](media/lab8_04.png)
  
1. On the **Access policy** pane, check all the available options under **Enable access to:** and click **Review + create**.

   ![](media/lab8_05.png)
   
1. On the **Review + create** page, review your configuration and click **Create**.

   ![](media/lab8_06.png)
  
1. Once the Key Vault deployment successfully finishes, you will be automatically redirected back to the **Select a key** panel. Click the **Create a new key** link.

   ![](media/lab8_07.png)
  
1. On the **Create a key** panel, fill in the following details:

   - **Options**: Select **Generate (1)**.
   - **Name**: Enter **app-key (2)**.
   - **Key type**: Select **RSA (3)**.
   - **RSA key size**: Select **2048 (4)**.
   - Leave all other values at their defaults and click **Create (5)**.

    ![](media/lab8_08.png)
  
1. Once the key is successfully generated, you will be returned to the **Select a key** panel. Click **Select**.

   ![](media/lab8_09.png)
   
1. Finally, click **Save** at the top of the Encryption pane and wait for the encryption changes to propagate. Remain on the Encryption pane and verify that your newly created customer-managed key is now actively displayed.

    ![](media/lab8_10.png)
